ABOUT THIS PROJECT

Curiosity, with evidence.

A movie recommendation and data-visualization project for the B.Y.T.E Data Science internship.

THE BUILDER

Jarrod Frank Tamargo

This project explores how collaborative filtering turns audience ratings into recommendations—and how clear visualizations explain the evidence and limitations.

GitHub profile

THE APPROACH

Learn shared preferences.

Biased matrix factorization learns a numerical profile for every sampled user and trained movie. A new viewer's ratings fit a temporary profile against the fixed movie factors. Visitor ordering also uses item similarities learned from training audience ratings, so liked and disliked films directly affect the shortlist.

Missing ratings are unknown, not zero-star reviews. Recommendations exclude known rated films and require at least 20 training ratings per candidate. A validation-selected support bonus balances the personal estimate with how widely a movie was rated; it can favor familiar popular titles.

Sources & provenance

MovieLens 32M — GroupLens. Original data contains 32,000,204 ratings and 87,585 movies. Its ratings end October 12, 2023. Our catalogue and results describe a smaller reproducible sample.

Educational, noncommercial demonstration. No live streaming availability, ticket prices or 2026 catalogue coverage is claimed. A few source entries may be series or other screen titles.

Your choices stay local

No sign-in is needed. Ratings stay in this tab's session storage and can be erased using Clear my ratings. There is no analytics or tracking code. Poster images load from MetaHub, which receives ordinary image requests; your entered ratings are not sent to that service.
